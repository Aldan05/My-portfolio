SOS Activation • Advanced Personal Safety & Emergency Response Android Application

SOS Activation – System Details Android Application

Developed by Aldan L.M. Gehon • Personal Safety & Emergency Response

Project Overview & Goal

SOS Activation is an Android-based personal safety application engineered to provide hands-free emergency activation and rapid assistance through multiple alert mechanisms. The project goal is to improve personal safety by enabling quick emergency communication, location sharing, and automated alert workflows when users may be unable to operate their phones normally.

End-to-End Emergency Response Lifecycle

Voice / Power / Shake Trigger Countdown & Strobe Fused GPS Tracker SMS Broadcast + Calls

Key Engineered Features

Multiple SOS Triggers

Voice wake-word detection (Picovoice Porcupine), rapid power-button presses, and accelerometer shake detection for instantaneous hands-free emergency response.

GPS-Based Location Tracking

High-accuracy coordinate fetching via Google Play Services Fused Location Provider with live Google Maps links dynamically embedded into alerts.

Emergency SMS Alerts

Automated background SMS dispatch with customized SOS distress text and real-time GPS coordinates broadcast to multiple trusted emergency contacts.

Sequential Emergency Calling

Intelligent Telephony API integration executing sequential phone calling through trusted contacts until an answered connection is established.

Flashlight Strobe & Countdown

High-intensity camera flashlight strobe signaling for visual distress location, paired with a full-screen cancellation countdown to prevent false alarms.

Firebase Phone Auth & OTP

Secure phone-number authentication with one-time passcode (OTP) verification for verified identity and trusted contact synchronization.

Customizable Settings & Wake Words

Fully customizable emergency distress messages, custom trigger wake words, vibration patterns, and sensor sensitivity controls.

10 Indian Languages Supported

Multilingual emergency SOS messages supporting 10 Indian languages to ensure immediate communication across diverse regional responders.
